# Outreach Kit — Ironwood Books

Prepared 2026-08-19 as materials for T3.2 (blog outreach) and general use whenever Evan is pitching the books to a third party. This is copy-paste material, not a task list — Evan chooses who to contact and when.

## Brand blurb (2 sentences)

Ironwood Books is a one-person press: Evan Paliotta writes philosophy and life-wisdom books for his son, and shares them free with any family that finds them useful. The catalog is two picture books for ages 4-8 (curiosity, and Aristotle's Golden Mean) and one nonfiction guide for teenagers.

## Per-book blurbs (~50 words each)

**The Adventures of the Curious Kid** (ages 4-8): A rhyming picture book about a boy who spends one day following his curiosity into a mountain, an ocean, and a dark forest that genuinely scares him. Ends with him finding a book that opens every door at once. Free ebook, $9.99 paperback on Amazon.

**The Curious Kid Meets Aristotle** (ages 4-8): The same character time-travels to ancient Athens, where Aristotle teaches him the Golden Mean: not too much, not too little. As far as we've found, one of the only picture books that teaches a real, named idea from an actual historical philosopher. Free ebook, $9.99 paperback on Amazon.

**The Ways of the World** (ages 14+): A father's short, direct guide for his teenage son on power, money, human nature, and building a life worth living, drawing on Marcus Aurelius through Naval Ravikant. Written to be argued with, not memorized. Free ebook, $9.99 paperback on Amazon.

## The free-ebook offer line

"Every ebook is free, always. No newsletter trap, no trial, just the PDF." Use this whenever a reviewer or blog might worry about being asked to pay or sign up for something ongoing.

## Review-copy pitch email (~120 words)

> Subject: A free picture book for [blog name]'s readers
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I write picture books for my son and publish them for other families. [Book title] is a rhyming picture book for ages 4-8 about [one-line hook — curiosity / Aristotle's Golden Mean]. I noticed [blog name] covers books like this, and thought it might be a fit.
>
> The ebook is free (no strings) if you'd like to read it before deciding whether to write about it: [book URL]. Happy to send a paperback too if that's more useful for photos or a read-aloud video.
>
> No pressure either way, and thanks for the work you do pointing families toward good books.
>
> Evan

## Target list

### Confirmed via live AI-answer citation (highest priority)
- **fivebooks.com** — expert-curated "best books" lists, cited directly by Perplexity for "best philosophy books for kids" on 2026-08-19. Pitch as a candidate for a future "best philosophy picture books" or "best books to raise a thinker" list.
- **doinggoodtogether.com** — parenting/values-focused book list site, also cited in the same live check.

### Researched 2026-08-20 — verify current submission process before pitching, sites change policies
**Best fit, pitch first:**
- **Home School Book Review** (homeschoolbookreviewblog.wordpress.com) — explicitly accepts print review copies, values/worldview-aligned. Email: homeschoolbookreview@gmail.com. Print books only, no ebooks — will need to mail a physical copy.
- **Imagination Soup** (imaginationsoup.net) — curated picture-book-by-topic lists, active 2025+. Contact via site's About/Contact page.
- **Bookworm for Kids** (bookwormforkids.com) — honest unpaid reviews, explicitly prefers electronic copies (PDF/ePub) — lowest friction, no shipping needed. Contact via site review policy page.
- **Literary Mama** (literarymama.com) — 800-1500 word reviews for a parenting audience, accepts indie. Submit via email, subject line "Reviews Submission" (literarymama.com/submissions).

**Also worth pitching:**
- **The Peaceful Press** (thepeacefulpress.com) — homeschool "living books" reviews and lists.
- **Mom Read It** (momreadit.com) — active picture-book review blog, no formal submission process found, contact via site.
- **Nothing But Picture Books** (nbpbreviews.com) — picture-book-only review site, contact form.
- **Read-Aloud Revival** (readaloudrevival.com) — Sarah Mackenzie's homeschool booklist resource, 135k subscribers. Contact form; ask about featuring in a booklist rather than a full review.
- **Cathy Duffy Reviews** (cathyduffyreviews.com) — long-running homeschool curriculum/book review site.
- **The Children's Book Review** (thechildrensbookreview.com) — explicitly accepts self-published/indie; also offers PAID guaranteed placement ($289-389) if free submission doesn't get picked up — free tier via growingreaders@thechildrensbookreview.com first.

**Checked and skipped:** Toppsta (traditional-publisher only), BookPage (no self-published), The Picture Book Review (site returned an error, may be inactive).
